# Orchestrator review — decision

Accepted with a correction in `findings.md`: two, not one, coarse-step SBM cuts have ARI 0 despite edge AUC around 0.995. I checked the 384 replay records and crossing frames, reconciled 136 slice rows against their 1,088 initializations, and verified the original, projected, symmetric, Mean and fixed Huber summaries. For all 69 first-hit frames the centered max plus the initial center magnitude is below 20; 65 stayed below that bound through the horizon. Four crossed the centered threshold later. The reported 2.26e−8 full-horizon recenter deviation exceeds the original 1e−8 tolerance; the matched readouts and mathematical projection identity are separate from that numerical check.

**Decision:** Projection is a diagnostic, and the symmetric variant is not a performance candidate: zero hits on this slice were accompanied by an unstable comparison with Mean across step sizes. Do not select a step size based on ARI or open confirmation. Proceed with the plan §2 minimum matrix and the block/label-free separation (`003-bridge-selectivity`). To explain high AUC but poor ARI, record the exact Kruskal bottleneck: the smallest bridge score minus the larger of the two within-block MST bottleneck scores. A positive gap requires both planted blocks to assemble before any bridge is considered; a negative gap means an early merge across truth, and exact ties depend on the cutter's tie order. This oracle diagnostic is computed after scoring, never used by the partitioner.
