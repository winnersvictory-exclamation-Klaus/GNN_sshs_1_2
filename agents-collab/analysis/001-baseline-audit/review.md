# Orchestrator review — decision

Accepted `findings.md`. I independently keyed the full t310 CSV by graph/force/scheme, recomputed paired differences and failure counts overall and by family, bridge count, and candidate survival, and checked the rank baselines and negative controls in the source archive. The 18 fully surviving graphs show a +0.3974 mean gain from filtering the candidate itself but a −0.0046 low-pass difference from Mean wave. This survival-defined subset is descriptive; it does not identify a causal effect of dropping failed runs. The archive's threshold was `not all(abs(u) < 20)`, so the reanalysis changes scoring, not detection. The 16/16 nonzero reconstructed-hold scores on rank negatives are graph-level summaries, not proof of valid partitions: every cut isolates one node.

**Decision:** 30/48 failed graphs breaches the 5% development gate. No accuracy confirmation, no new confirmation seeds, and no choice of a winning family. Run one plan E stability diagnostic on the implicated force, with OWA/odd-OWA as small diagnostic controls and one edge-symmetric local Huber comparison (`002-center-drift`). Removing the degree-weighted mean force is an isolating diagnostic, not a new partition method: for shift-invariant forces with identical initialization it leaves exact pairwise differences unchanged before failure censoring. The edge-symmetric comparison tests a separate question: whether local clipping can be retained without uncompensated endpoint forces. Constant weighted center does not guarantee bounded relative motion. Retain the planned Mean/fixed Huber and same-force diffusion matrix for any subsequent mechanism/discovery test; do not infer a wave benefit from 002.
